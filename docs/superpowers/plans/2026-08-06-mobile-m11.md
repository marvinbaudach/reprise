# M11 — three ways to start music, and one control instead of five

This package removes more than it adds. That is the point.

Today the phone can play the title list from the top, or open an album and tap
its first track. An artist tile does nothing at all — `ArtistRow` has no
`clickable`. There is no way to reach the music you like. And a rating is set
through five separate stars on a surface where the only question anyone asks is
"do I want this again".

## What ships

**An artist opens.** Tapping an artist shows their tracks — a flat list ordered
by album and track number, with the album in each row's subtitle (the artist's
own name is redundant there). The same `TrackRows` that already draws the title
list and an opened album, with its paging, its playing row and its badge. No
sections, no new list type: ordered by album it *reads* as an album sequence
already, and a sectioned list is a cost with its own failure mode.

**Artist and album get a play button.** Above the list, one control that plays
what is below it. "Tap the first track" stops being the only way.

**Favourites are the fourth chip**, beside Titles · Albums · Artists. The chip
row answers "which list am I looking at", and favourites are a list. Ordered by
artist, then album, then track number, so tracks from one record stay together —
and since a list plays exactly as it stands (below), that order is the listening
order.

**Everything plays in order.** No play button shuffles. Shuffle is the transport
button, which has set *and* read back its state since M3b; a play button that
shuffled some lists and not others would be a second, invisible shuffle rule.

**One heart replaces five stars.** In the sheet, in the dock, in the library
rows. Heart on is a rating of five; heart off is zero.

There is nothing to remember and no previous value to restore, because on this
phone a rating is never anything but 0 or 5:

- the scanner writes no rating — `INSERT INTO tracks` carries title, artist,
  album, year, duration, bitrate and times, and there is no POPM tag reading
  anywhere in the core;
- nothing syncs ratings back from the desktop (`ratings_back` is off in V1, and
  the MTP sync carries files, playlists, lyrics sidecars and podcasts).

So the dock's careful restore rule from M9b — *tap sets five, tap again puts the
previous value back* — has nothing left to restore and goes with the stars.

## What is removed

- the five-star row in the Now Playing sheet, and the dock's single star with
  its remembered previous value;
- the `N/5` badge in library rows, and with it the M9c setting *Show ratings in
  library rows* and its key `ui.mobile.library_rating`;
- every path that can write a rating other than 0 or 5.

## The defect that comes with the territory

**The system back gesture does not close an open album.** `closeAlbum` hangs on
a clickable row in the UI and nothing else; the only `BackHandler` in
`BrowseScreen` belongs to the settings overlay. Opening an album and swiping
back leaves the app. The artist view would inherit exactly that, so it is fixed
here: back closes an open artist or album, and only leaves the library when
neither is open.

## What the core already does

Nothing new is needed below the boundary. `BrowseFilter` carries `artist` and
`rating`, and `query_track_window_browsed`, `query_track_count_browsed` and
`query_track_ids_browsed` all take it. The Android boundary has `list_tracks`,
`list_albums`, `list_artists`, `list_album_tracks` and `search_tracks`; it needs
the filtered window and count, nothing more.

## Proof

Every test mutation-proven: break the mechanism, show the real red output,
restore it, show green. A test that also passes without the change does not
count.

**Assert on what is drawn where the claim is about drawing.** A heart has the
same two states a star had, and the same trap: Material Symbols keeps "filled"
on the variable-font axis `FILL`, so `favorite` and `favorite_border` resolve to
one glyph and both states paint the same pixels. `MaterialSymbolFillTest.kt` and
`LibraryRatingVisibilityTest.kt` are the shape that catches it — rasterise and
count, do not ask the semantics.

Cover at least: an artist tile opens that artist; the list is ordered by album
and track number; the play button plays the whole list from its first track and
replaces the queue; the favourites chip lists exactly the hearted tracks in the
stated order; hearting writes five and un-hearting writes zero, through to the
database; a heart drawn on differs from a heart drawn off in pixels; and back
closes an open artist or album rather than the app.

Lifecycle claims run through `MainActivity.onCreate` and
`ActivityScenario.recreate`, never through a directly wired composable — M7's
warning, M9a's answer. Say which path each test takes.

stars, and this is a decision about the phone's surface, not about the value.
